## Not confirmed by any free source (do not present as official)

- Standard 개조식 endings (`~함`, `~임`, `~할 것`): no rule text found. Treated as convention (wording.md EN4).
- 연호 form of a date; `별첨` vs `붙임` (only `붙임` is an official term); 취소선; spacing of `ㆍ`; spacing between a date and a time.

- The latest 편람 after the 2025 edition (none found; today is 2026-11-14).
